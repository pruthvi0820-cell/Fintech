# Sample files for testing FinTray without a broker account

Made-up quantities and prices, in the formats brokers export. Not real holdings.

- `holdings_example.csv` — upload in the sidebar ("Your holdings") to try **🧭 Portfolio health** and portfolio questions in Ask AI.
- `tradebook_example.csv` — in **🏦 Long-term → ⏳ Holding tracker**, choose "A tradebook CSV from my broker" and upload it. It has lots that are already long-term, one that turns long-term on 2026-11-21, one at a loss, and a sale this financial year.
